MEET RAÍZ

Where it all started. Donde todo empieza.

RAÍZ is Christian alt-rock with Latin roots. Overdriven electric guitar carries the hook, congas and timbales drive the groove, and one voice sings it all in two languages. Think Santana and Maná colliding with ’90s alt-rock.

The songs switch between English and español the way bilingual families actually talk, mid-sentence and without translating.

Raíz means root. The self-titled debut goes back to where faith starts, and it’s out everywhere September 26.
